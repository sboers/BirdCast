Guild-Based Calculation Architecture

Adaptive climate scaling and micro-habitat adjustments

Overview

Thresholds for wind speed tolerance, heat/cold stress, and precipitation are dynamically scaled based on geographic latitude and local baseline climate.

Functional Avian Guild Breakdown

1. Songbirds & Garden Birds (Passerines) High Canopy/Bush Sensitivity

Highly sensitive to high winds and rain. Peak vocal activity during golden hour dawn/dusk.

2. Insect Hunters (Swallows, Martins, Swifts) Barometric & Humidity Driven

Tracks lower atmospheric air pressure shifts and high humidity pushing insects near ground or water level.

3. Soaring Raptors (Hawks, Eagles, Vultures) Thermal Lift & Solar Angle

Calculates solar noon thermal updrafts, cloud cover transparency, and moderate wind speeds for optimal soaring.

4. Waterfowl & Coastal Birds (Gulls, Ducks, Herons) High Weather Tolerance

Robust against strong winds and rain; high activity during storm fronts and tidal feeding shifts in coastal/wetland habitats.

Habitat & Latitude Modifiers

Selecting a micro-habitat applies environmental multipliers (e.g., Woodland buffers wind for Songbirds; Coastal boosts Waterfowl & Gull activity while increasing wind exposure penalty for Passerines).
